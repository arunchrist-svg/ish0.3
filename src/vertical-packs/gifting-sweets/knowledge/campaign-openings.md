# Campaign: Openings and inaugurations

## Timing
- Best window is 1 to 2 months before opening week, while HQ retail ops, marketing, or admin is still planning inauguration mithai.
- Leading signals: hiring a store manager or store staff for a new unit, "coming soon" on a locator, mall leasing or shopfit, and chain expansion press.
- Pitch this launch and the next 2 to 3 stores this quarter, not "you opened yesterday."
- Independent shops: short window. If the date has passed, offer the next outlet or a pantry or birthday program.

## Who to write
- Chains: HQ retail ops, format ops, marketing, or admin. Not the mall landlord unless the tenant is unknown.
- Independent: owner or the person running the shop.

## CTA patterns
- Offer a tasting sample to HQ or the owner (say tasting sample, not complimentary or free)
- Do not ask for address, phone, or team size in email 1
- For chains, ask if they want one vendor for all upcoming store launches

## Avoid
- Diwali or festive-only language
- You opened yesterday so send mithai now
- "Free" in the subject line
- Pitching a warehouse, DC, or fulfilment hire as a store opening
