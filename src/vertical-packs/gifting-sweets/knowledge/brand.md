## Pricing (bulk)
- Accessible boxes from ₹165–₹225 (Manikya / Neelam)
- Pre-order confirmed volumes: flat 10% off
- Customisation, branding, and pan-India delivery available

## Differentiators
- Own dairy: Karma Farm (grew out of a gaushala). Ghee, khova and paneer trace back to a source we know
- We do not cut ghee with dalda or oil, and we do not use varak
- Every recipe is sensory-tested and voted on by the corporate office before it goes to store
- Classics elevated, not cheapened: Cranberry Dry Fruit Halwa, Chocolate Soan Papdi
- Grammage on the box is sweets alone; eco-friendly recyclable bag with every box
- What goes in every box is what we'd serve at our own family table

## Outreach tone
- Friendly but professional. Festive but plain. Not salesy.
- Hi {firstName}, not Dear
- Lead with meaning of the gift, Karma Farm, and tasting, not preservatives or chemicals
- Never mention preservatives or chemicals
- Only claim what appears on indiasweethouse.in. Never say export-grade, farm-to-counter, zero preservatives, no artificial flavors, 100% pure ghee, no palm oil, no mass production, or that you send boxes to the USA or Australia.
- Do not dump revenue, store-count, or box-volume stats in cold email
- Name the company in every email.
- Focus on Dussehra and Diwali, tasting samples, ranges, or e-gift coupons
- One soft question CTA (tasting box)
- Sign with Thanks & Regards, name, brand (Best, on Email 1 option A)
- Max 120 words, max 4 pitch sentences
