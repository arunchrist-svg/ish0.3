# Campaign: Year-round programs

## Focus
Standing HR and admin programs that run every month: birthdays, new joiners, pantry namkeen, appreciation, and vendor empanelment. No festival required.

## Birthday
Monthly mithai or namkeen for that month's people. Buyer is HR or admin. CTA: tasting sample for the next birthday batch.

## Onboarding
Welcome box for campus or bulk hiring. Buyer is HR or campus ops.

## Pantry
Recurring namkeen and mithai for pantry and meeting rooms. Buyer is admin or facilities. Talk GST, delivery cadence, and SKU list. Avoid culture-only language.

## Empanelment
Procurement rate card and tasting so they can call for any occasion (festival, opening, birthday). Mention MOQ, pan-India delivery, and sample evaluation.

## CTA patterns
- Offer a tasting sample (not complimentary or free)
- Ask if they want a short note on options
- Do not harvest address or headcount in email 1

## Avoid
- Seasonal-only language
- Diwali subject lines
- Multiple CTAs
