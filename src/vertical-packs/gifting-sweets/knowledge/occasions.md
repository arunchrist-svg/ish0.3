# Occasions (year-round sweet boxes)

Festive gifting stays a peak. Year-round volume comes from events we can find in public sources, and from HR programs that need no news hit.

## Events (detect)
- store_opening: new store, grand opening, inauguration, or an upcoming outlet (hiring, coming soon, mall lease). Pitch HQ before opening week, then the next stores this quarter.
- office_inauguration: new office, campus, or plant. Pitch desk or lobby mithai for inauguration week. Not a retail shop.
- foundation_day: company anniversary. Pitch employee and partner boxes that week.
- milestone: funding, IPO, certification, first export lot. Pitch celebration boxes.

Sweets mentioned in the post are a bonus. The company being the giver of the event is enough.
Tag openings as upcoming when the source is hiring, coming soon, or will-open. Tag recent only when it already opened.

## Programs (always-on, no sweep)
- birthday: monthly birthday mithai or namkeen for that month's people.
- onboarding: new-joiner welcome boxes.
- appreciation: recognition, appraisal, town-hall.
- pantry: recurring namkeen and mithai for pantry and meetings (admin budget).
- empanelment: tasting plus rate card so procurement can call for any occasion.

## Writer
Account detected occasion wins. Else the Write-time theme. Diwali copy only when both are unset and campaign is diwali_gifting.
CTA: tasting sample or a short call. Do not ask for address, phone, or headcount in email 1.
If a store opening date is already past, pitch the next outlet or a standing program, not you opened yesterday.
If timing is upcoming, write as a store coming up: inauguration mithai planning for HQ retail ops, plus the next 2 to 3 stores.
