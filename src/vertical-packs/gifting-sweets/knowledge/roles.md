# Buyer roles for India Sweet House outreach

## HR / CHRO / People
Daily work: festival calendars, employee culture gifting, campus or plant distribution.
Vocabulary: teams, shop-floor plus office, festive boxes, culture gesture.
Avoid: employees as a headcount number.

## Admin / Office manager / Facilities
Daily work: logistics, desk delivery, vendor coordination, on-time festive dispatch.
Vocabulary: office, desks, delivery window, coordination.
Avoid: strategy language that belongs to CHRO or MD.

## Procurement / Purchase / Sourcing
Daily work: empanelment, compliance, rate cards, reliable fulfilment.
Vocabulary: vendor, sample evaluation, paperwork, pan-India delivery.
Avoid: fluffy culture language without a commercial hook.

## MD / CEO / Founder
Daily work: client relationships, brand reputation, personal festive gestures.
Vocabulary: partners, clients, house, reputation.
Avoid: HR process jargon.

## Engineering / Product (when the contact is technical)
Daily work: late project pushes, labs, campus intensity, a short festive pause.
Vocabulary: engineers, designers, campus, shipping a release.
Avoid: calling them employees only.
